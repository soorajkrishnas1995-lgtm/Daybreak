Space Science

NASA and China's Mars orbiters together reveal how solar wind waves are stripping the planet's atmosphere

Simultaneous observations by NASA's MAVEN and China's Tianwen-1 orbiters show the solar wind generates rolling Kelvin-Helmholtz waves at the boundary of Mars' upper atmosphere, creating plasma clouds that carry atmospheric ions into space. The effect is concentrated on one side of the planet depending on the solar wind's electric-field direction, according to research published in Science Advances. Scientists say the mechanism is likely a major reason Mars transformed from a once-wetter, thicker-atmosphere world into today's cold, dry planet, offering a rare joint US-China data set on the same phenomenon.

Eating less protein could slow aging, a major review of 350+ studies finds

A Cell Metabolism review of more than 350 studies found lower protein intake may improve metabolism, cut inflammation and activate longevity pathways similar to calorie restriction, partly by raising the hormone FGF21. The effect is population-dependent -- sedentary adults may be over-consuming protein, while athletes and older adults likely still need more.

ScienceDaily

Study estimates human lifespan's outer limit at around 156 years, even without disease

Modeling by Russia's Skoltech Institute, combining four organ systems with cell types like neurons as bottlenecks, found somatic mutation accumulation alone would cap median human lifespan around 156 years (range 146-194), even if every age-related disease were cured. Researchers frame it as a thought experiment, not a near-term prediction.
