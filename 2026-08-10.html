Massive decade-long study finds heavy drinking raises death risk no matter what you drink

A study tracking more than 340,000 adults for over a decade found that heavy drinking increased the risk of death regardless of whether the alcohol consumed was beer, wine or spirits, challenging the widely held belief that wine carries fewer health risks than other alcoholic beverages. Researchers say the findings undercut long-standing marketing narratives around the supposed heart-health benefits of moderate wine consumption, adding to a growing body of evidence that no amount of regular heavy drinking is metabolically safe, regardless of the beverage's antioxidant content or perceived cultural status.
