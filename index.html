Climate · Research

Earth's long-term warming rate has accelerated to its fastest pace since records began, study finds

Analyzing five major global datasets and stripping out short-term noise from El Nino cycles, volcanic eruptions and solar variation, researchers at Germany's Potsdam Institute found the planet has warmed at roughly 0.35 degrees Celsius per decade over the past ten years -- nearly double the approximately 0.2-degree-per-decade rate seen from 1970 to 2015. Published in Geophysical Research Letters with better than 98% statistical confidence, it is the first robust evidence that global warming's underlying rate, not just year-to-year variation, is itself accelerating -- the fastest pace since instrumental records began in 1880.
