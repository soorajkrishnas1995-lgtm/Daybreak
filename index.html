Berlin · Clinical Trial

In a German-led ten-week clinical trial, a THC-based medication eliminated PTSD nightmares completely for more than a third of patients

Researchers from Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin and other German institutions found that dronabinol, a prescription medication containing THC, helped more than a third of patients with PTSD see their recurring trauma-related nightmares disappear completely after ten weeks of nightly treatment. The randomized trial enrolled 171 adults with PTSD and chronic nightmares, who received either dronabinol, in doses ranging from 2.5 to 15 milligrams, or a placebo before bed each night. The findings point to a promising new treatment option for a debilitating symptom that has few effective therapies.
